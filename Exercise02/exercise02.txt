-- model - Perplexity
---prompt "Explain the difference between Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning using one real-world example for each. Then explain how they are related to each other."

--response - - Artificial intelligence (AI): a broad goal to create systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Example: a customer-service chatbot that understands questions and provides helpful replies.

- Machine learning (ML): a way to achieve AI by letting systems learn from data rather than being explicitly programmed. Example: an email spam filter that learns to distinguish spam from legitimate emails from labeled examples.

- Deep learning (DL): a subset of ML that uses large neural networks with many layers to learn complex patterns from big datasets. Example: a self-driving car’s perception system that recognizes pedestrians and traffic signs from camera images.

How they relate:
- DL is a subset of ML, which is a subset of AI.
- All DL and ML systems are forms of AI, but not all AI involves ML or DL.
- DL typically requires more data and computation than traditional ML and often reduces the need for manual feature engineering.
